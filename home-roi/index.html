FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
What types of companies do you work with?
We work with startups, growing businesses, and established companies across various industries — from tech and retail to professional services. What matters most is your readiness to evolve and collaborate.
How is your consulting process structured?
Our process includes six stages: discovery, research, strategy development, implementation support, review, and long-term partnership. It's structured but flexible — tailored to your business and goals.
Do you offer one-time consultations or only long-term projects?
Both. Some clients come to us for targeted audits or workshops, while others engage us on multi-month strategic initiatives. We’ll recommend the right approach based on your needs.
How long does a typical project take?
It depends on the scope. A focused strategy sprint may take 2–4 weeks, while full-scale transformation programs can run for several months. We always set clear timelines up front.
Will we work directly with your team?
Yes. You’ll have direct access to our core consultants — no handoffs, no layers of account managers. We value open communication and real collaboration.
